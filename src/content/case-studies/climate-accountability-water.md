snapshot: 'Verification-ready climate MRV integrated with operational monitoring for an urban water utility.'
setup: 'A water utility piloting a low-carbon service-delivery model that the climate fund wanted to underwrite. Existing monitoring was operational, not climate-focused.'
complication: 'The utility could measure cubic metres and revenue. It could not measure tonnes of CO₂e avoided — and the climate fund would not sign off without that evidence.'
solution: 'We integrated a verification-ready MRV calculator with operational monitoring, mapped and automated activity data flows from field to dashboard, documented monitoring and verification protocols, and completed a value-for-money analysis.'
results:
  - 'Managers tracked climate and service outcomes in one view'
  - 'Verification evidence was organised and ready for external review'
  - 'Investment decisions were guided by cost and performance signals'
takeaway: "Climate MRV that lives in a separate spreadsheet fails at verification. MRV integrated with the operational dashboard is the version that survives the buyer's review."
