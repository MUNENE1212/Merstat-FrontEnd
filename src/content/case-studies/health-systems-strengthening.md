snapshot: 'Routine information systems prepared for analysis and visualisation, with remote monitoring for facilities under access constraints.'
setup: 'A multi-country health portfolio with routine information systems that produced data but not decisions. Facility managers had reports no one acted on.'
complication: 'Field access was restricted in some districts, facility staff had limited analytics capacity, and the decision-makers needed quality and coverage signals in days, not months.'
solution: 'We prepared routine information systems for analysis and visualisation, used remote monitoring with risk-based sampling, and combined retrospective baselines with contribution analysis.'
results:
  - 'Facility managers used timely signals on service quality'
  - 'Remote sites were included in oversight without heavy burden'
  - 'Evidence supported course corrections during implementation'
takeaway: 'Routine data is only useful when it reaches a manager with the authority and the format to act. Remote monitoring, with consent and ethics, extends oversight without exhausting field teams.'
