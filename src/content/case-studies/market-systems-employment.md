order: 10
snapshot: 'Rolling-baseline design and contribution analysis for a multi-country market systems programme, with TPM in hard-to-reach districts.'
setup: 'A multi-year MSD programme operating across two fragile contexts, with seasonality that made a single-shot baseline impossible and access challenges that limited routine verification.'
complication: 'Standard mid-term reviews had been completed, but the implementer and the funder disagreed on what the programme had changed. The methodology needed to handle seasonality, attribute contribution without overclaiming, and verify delivery where field teams could not travel.'
solution: "We designed a rolling baseline that captured seasonality, applied contribution analysis to clarify MERSTAT's role in observed outcomes, agreed outcome and impact multipliers with the partner, and ran third-party monitoring in hard-to-reach locations."
results:
  - 'Course corrections were made in the first cycle'
  - 'Delivery was independently verified and accepted by funders'
  - 'Jobs and income estimates were credible and repeatable'
takeaway: 'A baseline that ignores seasonality is a baseline you cannot defend. Rolling baselines, paired with contribution analysis and TPM, give both the implementer and the funder a story they can sign.'
