setup: 'A regional aquaculture programme with a strong youth-employment mandate. Existing reporting counted placements; it did not count quality.'
complication: 'Donor guidance was tightening on jobs reporting — direct, indirect, and induced jobs, with FTE and quality markers — and the implementing team did not have the tools to deliver it.'
solution: 'We built a jobs framework covering direct, indirect, and induced jobs, trained teams on sampling, digital data capture, and Power BI use, and produced a mixed-methods review.'
results:
  - 'Jobs reporting was consistent, defensible, and aligned to donor guidance'
  - 'Teams owned the tools and maintained dashboards after handover'
  - 'Contribution stories were stronger and supported management decisions'
takeaway: 'Jobs frameworks are not just about counting. With the right FTE and quality markers, they become the language that turns placements into policy.'
