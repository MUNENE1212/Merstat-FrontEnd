Merstat is a development partner that designs and delivers programs, strengthens systems, and provides independent monitoring to ensure accountability and learning.

We bring expertise in market systems development and employment, market systems resilience, climate MRV, and value-for-money frameworks. Our work also includes ESG frameworks and training, jobs measurement frameworks, and tools for capital mobilization and impact investing tracking.

Every solution mainstreams gender equality, poverty reduction, youth empowerment, climate adaptation, resilience, and disability inclusion — ensuring that impact is both measurable and sustainable.
