Merstat is a development partner that brings together program design, implementation, independent monitoring, and advanced evidence systems to drive sustainable and inclusive change. We work with governments, development partners, businesses, and communities to design solutions that are impactful today and resilient tomorrow.

Our mission is to build accountable, inclusive, and climate-smart systems that generate prosperity for people and enterprises — using innovation, partnerships, and rigorous evidence.

Our vision is a future where development, investment, and policy decisions are guided by credible evidence, inclusive values, and sustainable solutions.
